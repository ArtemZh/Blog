What the shape says

Governance is the only domain present in all five phases — it opens the project and closes it. Stakeholders is the only other domain that starts in Initiating. Scope, Schedule and Finance go from Planning straight into Monitoring & Controlling: the eighth edition gives them no Executing processes — they are planned and controlled, the doing belongs to the delivery team. Resources and Risk are the domains where the project manager both plans and executes.
