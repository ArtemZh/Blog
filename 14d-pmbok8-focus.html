Focus View

Five phases as five columns, in the order a project meets them, with the performance domains stacked inside each column. Click a process to see only what feeds it and what it feeds: everything unconnected fades away, the sources gather on its left and the targets on its right. Click it again, or press Esc, to get the whole sheet back.

The sheet

Click a process to focus on it — it stays exactly where it is and the rest gathers around it. Click a domain's colour panel to line that domain up as one band across the phases. The + in a card's corner opens its inputs and outputs. Click again, click empty canvas, or press Esc to return.

What the focus says

The full sheet is the map; the focused state is the answer to a single question — what does this process need, and who is waiting for it. Once the other thirty-odd cards are gone, the shape of a process is easy to read: some have a wide teal fan and a single clay arrow, some are the opposite, and the planning processes in the middle of the sheet tend to have both.

Sources keep their phase order left to right, so a card that pulls from Initiating sits further out than one that pulls from its own phase — the distance is roughly how far back in the project the input comes from.
