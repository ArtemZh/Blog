Phases Stacked

One phase per band, in the order a project meets them: Initiating, Planning, then Executing and Monitoring & Controlling side by side because they run at the same time, and Closing at the end. Inside each band the performance domains stand as columns, so a domain can be followed straight down the sheet.

What the stack says

Read downward, the sheet is a timeline: everything above a band has to have happened for that band to make sense, and the two middle bands sit side by side because execution and control are not consecutive — the project is being run and being watched at the same time.

Read down a column, it is the life of one domain. Governance is the only column that runs from the first band to the last. Scope, Schedule and Finance skip the Executing band entirely: they are planned and then controlled. Resources and Risk are the columns where the project manager both does the work and watches it.
